MAJOR CHANGES FROM PRIOR VERSION
- Complete re-edit to a 12-shot, 82-second pitch structure with
  rewritten, re-recorded narration (10 lines) answering what/why/how.
- All typography replaced with Inter (Display/Light/Regular/Medium/
  SemiBold), letter-spaced and consistently aligned. Old HUD removed.
- New minimal spatial UI: locality label, era labels with hairline
  rules, dot+leader anchor pins, ground-plane viewing-zone ellipse
  with activation pulse, 2-SYNCED badge. No panels, no cyan glass.
- Group PNGs deconstructed into 15 individually staged elements
  (settler figures, wagon+driver unit, campfire, ox, tipis, Dakota
  figures, mammoth, saber-toothed cat), each placed with
  perspective-derived scale, contact shadows, atmospheric wash by
  depth, and micro-motion (breathing, sway, walk drift, fire flicker
  with a light pool that flickers in sync).
- Occlusion: the 1873 wagon sits partially behind a feathered cut of
  the real stone ruins pile from the plate itself.
- Ice age fully redesigned: no duotone. Live falls cool gradually
  (white-balance shift + desaturation), snowfall and a natural grey
  haze bank rise, the falls freeze via a 3-second dissolve to a
  matched frozen frame, then fauna materialize at anchored scale.
- The three composite plates are the most tripod-stable windows found
  by motion analysis across all 34 clips; the Dakota plate runs at
  half speed to stay clear of park visitors.
- All real footage passes through one base grade (soft S-curve,
  highlight rolloff, exposure consistency); era grades are applied
  over plate+figures together so they share light.
- New product scene: hairline technical schematic of the glasses with
  four feature callouts (deliberate design-language choice — an honest
  schematic rather than a fake photoreal render).
- New Falls Park beta route map (5 experiences, 0.9-mile loop),
  restrained dark cartography.
- Audio rebuilt: real location ambience lifted from the iPhone clips
  (falls roar, park air), synthesized fire crackle / arctic wind /
  mammoth low-end / soft two-note UI tick / materialize shimmer, and
  a restrained felt-piano + air-pad score that builds by act and
  resolves under the end card. Mix loudness-normalized to -15 LUFS.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Historical figures use controlled micro-motion (breathing, sway,
  drift), not full character animation — no video-generation model is
  available in this environment.
- The glasses are presented as a design schematic, not a photoreal
  product render, for the same reason.
- A few distant park visitors remain visible in wide plates; they are
  small, and era grades reduce their presence.
- Narration is a neural voice (Andrew); a human VO session would be a
  drop-in replacement.
- The pipeline's automated QA flags the intentional dark product and
  end-card scenes as "black frames" — verified to be exactly those
  designed segments.
