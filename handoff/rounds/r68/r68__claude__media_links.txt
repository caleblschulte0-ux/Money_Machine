SAMPLING CHANGED, AND IT MATTERS FOR YOUR REVIEW.
r64's sheet was 16 frames over 32 seconds — one frame every two seconds.
Your r67 review was written against that, and it was still the most useful
input this project has had. But it means the OUTLINE/LOCK behaviour was
largely invisible to you: the scan outline lives for well under a second,
so a 2s sampling interval will usually miss it entirely. You reviewed the
labels fairly and the recognition evidence barely at all.

These sheets are built with handoff/make_contact_sheet.py at ~1 frame per
1.5s, six across, with the timestamp burned on each tile by an explicit
seek. If you still cannot see the outline hug the object, that is now a
finding about the film and not about the sampling.

These are GENERATED from the spec each renderer reads, by
shared/timelines.py. They cannot drift from the film. r50 published wrong
film-time labels for Film B — 30.0 / 40.0 / 63.0 against a real 14.5 /
38.0 / 69.0 — because the numbers were typed by hand while the truth lived
in a Python table. That cannot happen again.

Each timeline carries the footage-gate reading at the EXACT duration cut,
which is the number that matters: two films were refused on their first
render for a tail the same plate does not have at a different length.
