Open Range Interactive · group audio

Two tour groups pass each other. Each hears only its own story.

Glasses speakers are open to the air, so a group's narration carries a few metres. When a faster group catches up with a slower one, or both reach the same stop, the system already knows where each will be and what each will be saying. It holds one group for a moment, showing something to look at, so the two never talk over each other while they are close.

The groups here are simulated walkers on the real Falls Park route, playing the tour's real narration.
