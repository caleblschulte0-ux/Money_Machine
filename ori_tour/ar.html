Figures that stay in one spot

  1. Test mode: pick a figure, point at the ground, tap "Spawn here". It stands there at true size.
  2. "Set test point here" saves the spot. Walk away and come back: it reappears on its own.
  3. "Walk the tour here" drops the three Falls Park stops around you, with each stop's figure.
How to judge it
  1. Circle it slowly, all the way round. It should not slide; "corrected" in the readout stays under about 10 cm.
  2. Walk 50 m away and come back. It should be where you left it.
  3. Lock the screen, unlock, tap "Back to AR". A saved test point's figure comes back near the same spot (a web page cannot keep the exact spot).
  4. In the sun: its shadow should fall the same way as yours, and it should not look brighter or darker than things around it.
  5. Walk so a car or a person is between you and it: where this phone senses depth, they hide it.

In a parking lot: watch for cars, and look up from the screen while you walk.
