# Customer

## Who buys

Marketing leads, founders and heads of growth at 20–200 person companies that
sell considered purchases — professional services, B2B software, specialty
manufacturing, regional service firms with a national reputation.

The buyer already invests in being findable. They have an SEO budget, or they
had one, and they have noticed that some of their traffic and some of their
buyers' questions have moved.

## The situation

They know buyers ask AI assistants for recommendations. They have typed their
own company name into one and been unsettled by the answer — or by its absence.
They have no measurement, no baseline and no idea whether it changed last month.

Their agency does not know either, and says so in more words.

## What they say

- "A prospect told me they asked ChatGPT and we weren't mentioned."
- "I don't know if this matters yet, but I don't want to find out in a year."
- "Our competitor comes up and I don't know why."
- "Is this just SEO again?"

The last one is the important objection. The honest answer is: partly, and the
part that is different is that nobody can promise you a position.

## Who does not buy

| Not a fit | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Wants guaranteed placement | We cannot deliver it and will not claim it |
| No existing search investment | Not yet feeling the problem |
| Pure local services | Local pack and maps still dominate; less exposure |
| Regulated verticals | Answer accuracy carries risk we are not equipped for |

Declining the first category is a positioning decision, not a lost sale — that
customer will churn angry.

## How they are reached

1. Existing automation agency clients — warm, and already trust us
2. Content demonstrating the measurement on well-known brands
3. Marketing communities where the anxiety is already being discussed
4. Partnering with SEO agencies who lack the capability

## What earns trust

Showing them their own data in the first conversation, including the
uncomfortable parts: where a competitor appears instead, and where the answer
about them is simply wrong. And saying explicitly, before they ask, that no
placement can be guaranteed.
