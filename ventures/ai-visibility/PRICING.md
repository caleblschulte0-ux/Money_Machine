# Pricing

| Offer | Price | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-Time Audit | $1,500 | one-time |
| Monthly Monitoring | $900 | monthly |
| Visibility Growth Program | $3,500+ | monthly (planned) |
| Enterprise Brand Monitoring | quoted | monthly (planned) |

## Reasoning

**$1,500 for the audit** sits below the threshold requiring committee approval
for the target buyer, and above the level at which someone buys out of idle
curiosity.

**$900/month for monitoring** is priced against what the buyer already pays for
rank tracking and reporting tools, not against a savings claim. It is a
measurement subscription and is priced like one.

**The growth program** is priced on content production, which is real labour.

## The margin constraint

`monitoring_cost_per_client` above 30% of the monthly price is a kill
criterion. At $900/month that is $270 of observation and analysis cost — which
bounds how many questions and engines can be covered per client. That
constraint should be visible in the contract as a named question set, not
absorbed silently.

## What is not done

- **No performance pricing.** Charging on appearance rate would mean charging
  for something we do not control, which is exactly the promise we refuse to
  make.
- **No annual prepayment** until retention is known.
- **No discounting to close.** A buyer who needs a discount to test a $900
  measurement subscription is not feeling the problem.

## Review triggers

- Renewal below 60% at month three — a kill criterion; likely the report is not
  actionable rather than mispriced
- Monitoring cost above 30% of price — reduce scope or raise price
- Close rate above 50% — priced too low
