# Age rating — draft answers

Not legal advice. The Kids Category decision (docs/PRIVACY.md §7) is open and
changes the review posture; these are the questionnaire answers as the
software stands.

- Cartoon or fantasy violence: **None**
- Realistic violence: **None**
- Sexual content / nudity: **None**
- Profanity or crude humor: **None** (Barkly is deadpan and blunt; the writing is checked to stay child-appropriate)
- Alcohol, tobacco, drugs: **None**
- Mature/suggestive themes: **None**
- Horror/fear themes: **None**
- Medical/treatment info: **None**
- Gambling / contests: **None** — park "contests" are fetch races with no stakes
- Unrestricted web access: **No** — the only links open a privacy and a support page, behind a parental gate
- User-generated content: the child's own speech is shown back only to that child on that device; nothing is shared with other users. **No** sharing features.
- In-app purchases: **None**
- Advertising: **None**

Expected result: **4+**. Note in the submission that the app contains an AI
dialogue feature whose replies are generated; the offline fallback and the
child-appropriate prompt rules are described in docs/PRIVACY.md §3.
