Barkly is not an AI chatbot shaped like a dog. He is a dog who becomes different because of what you do together.

TEACH HIM
Say "when I say showtime, spin, then play dead" and it's his forever. Say the word later and he does it, no internet needed. The dumber the routine, the better the private joke.

HE REMEMBERS
Tell him your favourite food. Come back tomorrow and ask. He keeps a diary of what you two actually did — the walk, the dig, the day the rival dog called the good stick a twig — and he'll tell you about it, in his own words.

THE PARK HAS POLITICS
Biscuit is a friend. Duke is a problem. See them enough and it becomes history: a best friend, a nemesis, a feud with chapters. Your choices at the park change how Barkly talks about them at home.

THE PACK BOOK
What kind of Barkly did you make? Lore Hoarders. Certified Menaces. Dirt-Digging Legends. It's not a quiz — it's derived from what happened. Show it to a friend; theirs will be different.

HE HAS INITIATIVE
Sometimes he bothers you for a reason. "You said we'd play. That was yesterday. I remember things." "We have five treasures now. That's a collection."

DESIGNED FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• The microphone listens only while the TALK button is held — no wake word, never in the background.
• Audio never leaves the phone; only the words do, so he can answer.
• No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, no in-app purchases.
• A parent screen explains exactly what is kept and lets you delete all of it, behind a parental gate.

Barkly works with no account. Everything he knows about you lives on your phone.
