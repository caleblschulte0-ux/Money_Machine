# Barkly Home quality bar

Home is the production visual benchmark for the rest of Barkly.

- Barkly is the emotional hero, but visible authored world must frame him on both sides.
- Furniture and architecture are modular code-owned assets, never one baked room image.
- Rendered assets use one camera/light/material language and are alpha-trimmed before promotion.
- The live window keeps time-of-day scenery in code behind a rendered physical frame.
- Wall, floor, foreground and props must read as distinct depth planes on a 390x844 phone.
- No UI/world/NPC collisions across the supported responsive audit sizes.
- Do not propagate this art direction to Park/Town/Beach until the Home phone screenshot clears this bar.
